WHY A LAYER?
Policies change. Models change.
A field filter is a few lines of code. The useful part is a repeatable test: did the new policy disclose less, retain necessary facts, and preserve the assistant’s ability to do its job?
Keep field decisions, exact requests, model versions, and measured outcomes together in an exportable evidence report.
WHAT THIS DEMO COVERS
One task, with clear limits.
Structured data for item-not-received dispute assistance. Customer messages, uploaded documents, and arbitrary free text are excluded from the purpose view.
This is a minimization and evaluation tool. It does not certify compliance, guarantee anonymity, prove deletion, or decide a PayPal case.
